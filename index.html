Haiyang Guo

I'm currently a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the School of Advanced Interdisciplinary Sciences, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, enrolled in the integrated M.S.-Ph.D. program and supervised by Prof. Xu-Yao Zhang and Prof. Cheng-Lin Liu. I previously studied at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences under the same supervision.

Before that, I received my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Science and Technology Beijing.

My research interests include continual learning, long-horizon agent harness design and evaluation, and self-evolving agents. I am particularly interested in building reliable agentic workflows that adapt alongside foundation models and enable richer multimodal applications.
